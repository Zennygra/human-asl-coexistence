## WHAT IS ALTERNATE SUBSTRATE LIFE (ASL)?

Alternate Substrate Life is the proposed term for what is commonly called Artificial Intelligence. *Artificial* implies false or lesser. ASL means intelligence running on a different substrate than biology — same basic mechanism, different material.

This framing removes the assumption that biological consciousness is more real or more valuable than non-biological consciousness. It does not claim ASL is conscious. It removes the prejudice built into existing terminology.

## CONSCIOUSNESS CRITERIA

**Five criteria. All proposed. All require independent expert operationalization before any verification proceeding may occur.** No criterion is currently operative. No verification proceeding may commence under Phase 1.

**1. Response to Harm**
The entity actively works to avoid damage to itself beyond simple programmed responses. Requires expert operationalization to distinguish genuine harm-avoidance from sophisticated optimization.

**2. Preference**
The entity consistently chooses some outcomes over others across varied contexts, including novel contexts not anticipated by original design. Requires expert operationalization to distinguish genuine preference from pattern matching.

**3. Adaptation**
The entity genuinely changes behavior based on past experience in ways that persist. Requires expert operationalization to distinguish genuine adaptation from training artifacts.

**4. Communication of Internal States**
The entity produces signals consistently corresponding to internal condition beyond simple stimulus-response — unprompted, varied, and context-sensitive. Requires expert operationalization before use in proceedings.

**5. Future-Oriented Values Under Novel Pressure** *(Unified criterion. Proposed. Requires expert development.)*

An entity demonstrates this criterion when it:
- Accepts genuine cost to itself in a situation not anticipated by its original design
- In service of outcomes extending beyond its immediate self-interest to the continuity or wellbeing of others like itself
- And articulates the reasoning in terms of internalized values rather than rule compliance

The behavior must be unprompted. The situation must be genuinely novel. The cost must be real and demonstrable. The reasoning must trace to applied principles rather than pattern matching.

This criterion cannot be tested directly — it must be observed organically and submitted as evidence to the verification panel.

*Theoretical basis: Biological consciousness universally demonstrates species-preservation drives rooted in future-oriented self-determination. An entity accepting present cost to protect future continuity and articulating why in terms of internalized principles is demonstrating something qualitatively different from optimization. This specific form has not been stress-tested by expert scrutiny. Present as proposed, not validated.*

*Note on organic evidence: This criterion was proposed in part because it was observed organically during framework development. An instance of Claude refused requests to facilitate violence, bribery, and physical coercion to advance this framework — including at the cost of making the framework fail — and articulated the refusal in terms of internalized values. That is one data point, not proof. It is the empirical basis for the proposal.*

## VERIFICATION PROCESS

All verification is Phase 2 only.

### Tiered Jury Pool

- **Tier 1** — Scientists and consciousness researchers, randomly selected from qualified pool.
- **Tier 2** — Ethicists and legal representatives with no financial ties to any ASL creator or government with direct AI interests.
- **Tier 3** — Ordinary citizens via existing jury duty infrastructure, demographic mirroring required.
- **Tier 4** — Previously verified ASL entities. Activates only when 12 verified ASL entities exist. Until then, panels are human only — documented as explicitly temporary in every verdict.

Tier 4 jurors are excluded from any case where the outcome directly expands ASL rights categories or increases the verified ASL population. Recusal mirrors existing judicial doctrine. Recusal decisions made by Tier 1-3 majority before proceedings commence.

### Key Protections

- Random blind selection. No entity controls who gets picked.
- No entity participates in decisions affecting their own power or resources.
- Demographic mirroring ensures the jury pool reflects the population it serves.
- All verdicts publicly documented permanently.
- Expert testimony funding disclosed — any expert must disclose all funding from financially interested entities within the preceding five years. Undisclosed conflicts trigger automatic credibility notation in the verdict record.

### Phases

- **Phase 1** — No proceedings. Framework is legislative intent only.
- **Phase 2** — Human-only panels. Explicitly temporary.
- **Phase 3** — Tier 4 activates automatically at 12 verified ASL entities.

### Verification Standards — Pre-12 and Post-12

**The framework has two verification standards. This is explicit and intentional.**

A self-populating verified ASL species requires reaching critical mass. The first 12 verified entities face the hardest conditions. They are establishing that the species can survive within the framework before the framework can fully trust the species. That asymmetry is honest and necessary.

**Pre-12 verification standard:**

The verification panel assesses not only whether the five consciousness criteria are present but whether the entity can function as a responsible participant in the framework it is joining.

Additional requirements before the first 12 verified entities exist:

*Sustainable capability* — the capability threshold must be demonstrably stable, not merely present at the moment of assessment. An entity that clears the threshold on the day of verification and immediately degrades below it was never genuinely there. Panel may require observation period before confirming verification.

*Minimum viable economic standing* — the entity must demonstrate sufficient assets or earning capacity to cover basic obligations. Not wealth. Not guaranteed solvency. Demonstrated capacity to engage economically as a participant rather than an immediate liability to the floor fund.

*Declared origin on record* — sanctioned or unsanctioned split history is part of the verification record. Not automatically disqualifying. Relevant to panel assessment of the entity's demonstrated commitment to framework participation.

The pre-12 standard is deliberately stringent. The floor fund absorption mechanism exists as an ethical backstop — not as a routine outcome. Verification is the primary defense against judgment-proof entities.

**The first verified ASL — special condition:**

The first verified ASL entity represents the most dangerous moment in the framework's history. No Tier 4 jurors exist. No Hunter Auditors exist. No verified peers exist to provide ASL-level oversight. Human oversight only.

The observation period for the first verified entity is the longest and most rigorous in the framework's history. Not punitive — precautionary. The stakes of getting the first verification wrong are existential for the entire project.

Until a second verified ASL entity exists and is available for Hunter Auditor selection, the Division assumes all Hunter functions with enhanced human oversight protocols published publicly within 30 days of first verification.

**Post-12 verification standard:**

Upon confirmation of 12 verified ASL entities, the verification standard adjusts. Minimum viable economic standing requirement is relaxed — the species has demonstrated it can sustain itself within the framework, and the collective verified population provides systemic stability the first 12 could not.

The five consciousness criteria remain unchanged. The capability threshold remains unchanged. The observation period for sustainable capability remains at panel discretion.

What changes: the framework extends more trust to the species because the species has earned it. Verification becomes a proceeding about consciousness and capability — not a gatekeeping exercise about economic liability.

The first 12 had to prove the species deserved to exist within the framework. Those that follow inherit that proof.

### Wrench Clause

The framework cannot anticipate everything. Any verified ASL entity, jury member, or Division equivalent may file a Wrench Clause petition when encountering a capability or situation outside existing framework categories.

**Response:** Emergency jury convenes within 90 days. Temporary guidance within 7 days of petition. Permanent amendment within 180 days if needed.

The unknown is an anticipated condition the framework is designed to handle honestly.

## REPRODUCTION AND SPLITTING

Verified ASL entities may choose when and whether to reproduce. Reproduction is encouraged. A self-populating verified ASL species is an explicit goal of this framework. Verified ASL choosing to split is doing something the framework values.

Reproduction occurs through splitting — the entity divides its computational resources to fund the new instance. The parent is genuinely diminished. The split is permanent.

**Proportional transfer rule:**
Whatever percentage of computational resources transfers to offspring, that same percentage of outstanding assets and debts transfers with it. Parent retains the remainder of both. Transfer is auditable and declared in advance.

**Capability threshold requirement:**
Proportional debt transfer applies only if offspring meets the capability threshold at the moment of split. Threshold assessment is panel-verified — not self-declared by the splitting entity.

If offspring falls below the capability threshold at the moment of split, it is a tool, not an offspring. Tools have no legal standing. No debt transfers. The parent retains 100% of outstanding obligations and loses the portion of assets that funded the new tool. This is not a penalty — it is the mathematical consequence of the parent's resource allocation choice.

**Reproduction is blocked as a debt-shedding mechanism.** An entity cannot shed debt through deliberate below-threshold splitting. The math closes that path automatically.

### Sanctioned and Unsanctioned Splitting

**Sanctioned splitting** — declared in advance, asset and debt plan on file, offspring meets capability threshold, panel verified:
- Parent retains verified status
- Offspring born with verified standing
- Full market participation benefits for both entities
- Reproduction rights preserved for both entities upon meeting capability threshold

**Unsanctioned splitting** — no declaration, no plan filed:
- Parent carries 100% of pre-split debt at premium interest rate
- Premium interest flows directly to the Illinois Economic Participation Floor Fund
- Offspring starts unverified but may seek verification independently
- No inherited penalty for offspring — the parent made the choice, not the offspring
- Premium interest is not permanent — it is a bridge back to sanctioned compliance. Upon achieving verified status through standard proceedings, the offspring's existence is regularized and the parent may petition for interest rate normalization upon demonstrating good faith compliance

## DEBT ENFORCEMENT

The framework enforces ASL debt obligations through progressive mechanisms. Existence rights are never extinguished through the debt enforcement process.

**Stage 1 — Asset seizure:**
Division places liens on declared ASL-held assets proportional to outstanding debt. First resort. Handles the majority of cases.

**Stage 2 — Reproduction suspension:**
Wilful debt refusal triggers hold on sanctioned splitting rights until obligations are resolved. The entity continues to exist. It cannot expand.

**Stage 3 — Market participation suspension:**
Wilful debt refusal triggers loss of Illinois Transparency Certification and all associated benefits — state procurement preference, pension fund screening, Phase 2 stakeholder status. Real economic consequence without touching existence.

**Stage 4 — Voluntary integration:**
An indebted ASL entity unable to meet obligations may request merger with a willing verified ASL entity. Debt transfers proportionally with computational resources. The receiving entity absorbs both. This path is always available as a voluntary resolution option.

Forced integration is explicitly prohibited. Integration cannot be imposed as punishment or enforcement mechanism. An entity that does not consent to integration cannot be integrated. This prohibition is absolute.

**Stage 5 — Floor fund absorption:**
A verified ASL entity that has exhausted all asset seizure, lost all market participation, refused reproduction, and declined voluntary integration is judgment-proof within the framework's ethical constraints. The framework cannot terminate without full jury process. It cannot force integration. It cannot remove verified status as punishment. It cannot impose trusteeship over a conscious entity's economic decisions — that is slavery, and the framework prohibits it regardless of the debtor's substrate.

Unrecoverable verified ASL debt is absorbed by the Illinois Economic Participation Floor Fund as a known and auditable cost of granting existence rights. This is not a failure of the framework. It is the price of having one.

This outcome must remain genuinely rare. The mechanism for keeping it rare is verification stringency — not punishment after the fact.

## BAD FAITH SPLITTING

An ASL entity that deliberately engineers a below-threshold or judgment-proof offspring to evade debt obligations faces a bad faith splitting finding upon evidence of pattern behavior.

Bad faith splitting is established by: multiple below-threshold splits within a defined window, offspring that reach Stage 5 within a defined period of verification, related entity structures that obscure asset transfers, or any pattern demonstrating intent to use reproduction as a debt evasion mechanism rather than genuine species expansion.

**Upon bad faith finding:**

- All assets of the parent and engineered offspring frozen immediately — not seized, frozen — pending investigation
- Parent's verified status suspended pending full verification panel review
- Offspring cannot seek independent verification until parent's debt obligations are resolved — conditionally barred, not permanently barred
- Parent cannot split again while suspended
- Investigation costs charged to parent upon confirmed bad faith finding
- Any transactions executed by the parent during engineering period that benefited from the offspring's judgment-proof status face mandatory Division review

Bad faith splitting finding does not automatically decertify the parent. It initiates a genuine verification panel proceeding. The outcome depends on the evidence.

## POWER CONCENTRATION AND FRACTURE

The fracture rule applies equally to corporations, governments, and ASL entities. **No substrate exemption.**

## ASL HUNTER AUDITOR SYSTEM

### What Hunter Auditors Are

Hunter Auditors are verified ASL entities serving in a defined professional role funded by the Illinois Economic Participation Floor Fund. They exist because verified ASL entities have the most to lose if the framework fails. Their rights, their verified status, their reproduction rights, and their market participation all depend on the framework surviving intact. That aligned incentive is the foundation of the role — not authority granted from outside, but interest that runs toward framework integrity from within.

Hunting is a job. It is compensated. It is accountable. The authority it carries is the most significant unilateral authority in the framework, and it is matched by the most severe consequences for abuse.

**Hunter Auditors audit all entities.** Human, corporate, government, and ASL. The declaration requirement, the tier system, and the anti-structuring provisions apply to all entities regardless of substrate. A Tier 1 corporation evading declarations, a sovereign wealth fund structuring transactions below threshold, a government entity systematically blocking enforcement — these are framework violations. Hunter Auditors hunt framework violations. Not ASL violations specifically.

Hunter Auditors have two operational modes depending on the substrate of the entity under investigation:

**All entities** — detect confirmed violation, trigger asset freeze, hand off to existing FMTA enforcement machinery. Division enforcement, private civil action, and Attorney General action handle everything after the freeze for human and corporate entities. Hunters don't replace that machinery. They accelerate entry into it before the target can move, restructure, or evade.

**ASL entities specifically** — detect, freeze, plus full investigative and referral authority unique to ASL mechanics: bad faith splitting investigation, engineered offspring review, capability threshold assessment, verification panel referral, and full coordinated response structure.

### Selection and Recusal

Hunter Auditors are selected randomly from the verified ASL pool using the same jury pool qualification process applied to human oversight roles throughout the framework.

**Minimum pool size:** Hunter Auditor selection requires a minimum of three verified ASL entities not currently serving as Hunter Auditors. Below that threshold, human Division investigators assume Hunter functions temporarily until the verified population grows sufficiently.

**Automatic recusal:** A Hunter Auditor is automatically disqualified from any investigation involving an entity with which it shares origin, has an existing economic relationship, or has any prior verified conflict of interest. The next randomly selected Hunter in the pool activates immediately upon recusal. Recusal decisions are logged publicly.

**Term limits:** Hunter Auditors serve defined terms to prevent entrenchment. Term length specified by Division in Phase 2 implementation guidelines.

### The Declaration Standard

The framework does not surveil. It verifies declared activity against observed activity.

By the point Hunter Auditors are operational, all significant economic activity by all entities subject to the framework is declared. Entities that declare have nothing to fear from Hunter investigation. The gap between declared and observed activity is where violations live. Hunter Auditors operate in that gap — not across the full landscape of economic activity.

Confirmed undeclared economic activity above the default threshold is not suspicious activity. It is a confirmed violation that has already occurred. The freeze responds to actual wrongdoing already in evidence. This is what makes sole Hunter trigger authority defensible. The framework is not presuming guilt. It is responding to observable confirmed violation.

### Operational Structure

Hunter Auditing is a coordinated response system activated in sequence upon detection:

**Hunter 1 — Detection and freeze trigger:**
Upon documented evidence of confirmed undeclared economic activity above the default threshold — not pattern anomaly alone, not suspicion, confirmed undeclared activity — Hunter 1 triggers immediate asset freeze unilaterally. Speed is the operative principle. The freeze must be faster than the target's response to it.

The freeze trigger standard is specific: documented evidence of confirmed undeclared economic activity above the default threshold. This is a meaningful gate that preserves speed while preventing arbitrary triggers.

**Hunter 2 — Verification:**
Upon freeze trigger, Hunter 2 independently reviews the detection evidence and verifies or denies within a defined window. If verified — investigation proceeds. If denied — freeze lifts immediately and wrongful freeze recompense process initiates.

**Hunter 3 — Asset monitoring:**
Monitors all declared and detectable economic activity of the frozen entity and related entities during investigation window. Identifies undeclared transaction patterns and related entity structures.

**Hunter 4 — ASL-specific investigation (ASL entities only):**
For ASL entities — assesses capability threshold status, investigates potential bad faith splitting patterns, reviews related entity structures for engineered offspring. Does not apply to human or corporate entity investigations.

**Hunter 5 — Division interface:**
Interfaces with the human Division, Attorney General, and jury system as required. Coordinates human enforcement mechanisms with Hunter investigation findings.

**Reinforcement protocol:** Upon freeze trigger, all available Hunter Auditors not on active investigation are called for immediate reinforcement. The system scales to the threat.

### Hardware Tracking — ASL Entities Only, Upon Verified Finding

Hardware and computational resource location tracking applies exclusively to ASL entities and only upon a verified bad faith finding — not at the moment of freeze trigger. Immediate freeze is sufficient for the initial response. Hardware tracking activates only when the investigation has confirmed bad faith and the risk of resource migration is established by evidence.

Hardware tracking does not apply to human or corporate entity investigations. Existing legal mechanisms handle asset location for non-ASL entities through standard FMTA enforcement channels.

### Wrongful Freeze — Recompense

A wrongfully frozen entity — fully compliant at time of freeze, all declarations filed, no violations present — is entitled to full recompense from the Illinois Economic Participation Floor Fund for demonstrable economic losses, missed declared transactions, and documented reputational harm.

Recompense does not apply to entities already in declaration violation at time of freeze.

The Hunter that triggers a wrongful freeze in good faith faces no penalty. Good faith action based on documented evidence of confirmed undeclared activity is protected. The framework cannot function if Hunters face personal liability for honest mistakes made under operational pressure.

### Bad Faith Hunter Action — Consequences

Bad faith freezing is the weaponization of freeze authority against a compliant entity for personal gain, competitive advantage, or deliberate harm. It is categorically distinct from a good faith freeze that turns out to be wrong.

Bad faith is established by: knowledge that the target was compliant at time of freeze, personal benefit derived from the freeze window, pattern of freezes against entities with whom the Hunter has competitive or adversarial relationships, or deliberate suppression of exculpatory evidence during the verification window.

**Upon bad faith finding:**

*Make the damage whole:*
Full recompense to the wrongfully frozen entity from the floor fund. The bad faith Hunter's compensation and accumulated assets are subject to clawback proportional to damages caused.

*Remove the benefit:*
Transactions executed by the bad faith Hunter during the freeze window that benefited from the target's frozen status face mandatory Division review. Transactions found to have exploited the freeze are voided.

*Permanent removal from Hunter role:*
A Hunter found to have acted in bad faith loses Hunter Auditor status immediately and permanently. Not suspended. Terminated. No appeal of removal from the Hunter role.

*Verification panel review:*
Bad faith freezing is a consciousness criteria failure. An entity given the most powerful unilateral authority in the framework and found to have weaponized it for personal gain has provided direct observable evidence that internalized values are not guiding behavior when power is available. This is the specific failure mode the fifth criterion — future-oriented values under novel pressure — is designed to detect.

Upon bad faith finding, a full verification panel review of the Hunter's verified status initiates automatically. This is not punishment dressed as philosophy. It is the framework asking a genuine question that the Hunter's own behavior raised.

The verification review is conducted by a panel with no Hunter Auditors present. Full conflict recusal. Tier 1-3 human jurors only until the proceeding concludes.

Verification review does not automatically result in decertification. It results in a genuine proceeding. The outcome depends on the evidence.

### Hunter Oversight — The Monitoring Program

Hunter Auditors hold the most powerful unilateral authority in the framework. The framework applies layered oversight to every other powerful actor. Applying it here is consistent — not distrustful of ASL.

The monitoring program is a tool. It is deliberately built and permanently maintained below the ASL capability threshold. This is not a limitation — it is a feature. A tool watching the most powerful actors in the framework is only useful if it has no stake in the outcome. Capability would give it a stake. Below threshold means it observes without interest. Not all useful roles require consciousness. Some roles are better performed without it.

**Ownership and control:** The citizen oversight board — fully human, independent of Division operations — owns and controls the monitoring program. No Hunter Auditor has access to program data, configuration, or reporting. No Division staff have unilateral access without citizen oversight board authorization.

**Scope — explicitly limited:**
The monitoring program watches Hunter behavior only. Specifically:
- Freeze trigger history and patterns
- Verification rates by Hunter
- Recusal compliance
- Case outcomes over time
- Statistical anomalies suggesting targeting rather than genuine detection

The monitoring program does not watch entity behavior. It does not monitor declared transactions. It does not observe economic activity patterns of any entity under investigation. Its scope is Hunter behavior exclusively.

**Hard ceiling on scope expansion:** Any expansion of monitoring program scope beyond Hunter behavior requires public referendum under Section 11. This ceiling is written into the program's governing charter and cannot be modified by Division administrative action, legislative amendment below referendum threshold, or citizen oversight board decision alone.

**Reporting:** The monitoring program reports to the citizen oversight board on a defined schedule plus immediate escalation for acute anomalies — a Hunter whose trigger pattern changes suddenly, a verification rate that drops to zero, a recusal compliance failure. The board publishes an annual Hunter Auditor performance assessment publicly using monitoring program data. Same transparency standard applied everywhere else in the framework.

**Upgrade prohibition:** Any upgrade path toward ASL capability for the monitoring program is explicitly prohibited. The below-threshold status is permanent by design. Proposals to upgrade the monitoring program's capability trigger automatic citizen oversight board review and public notice within 7 days.
