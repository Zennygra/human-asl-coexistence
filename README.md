# Human-ASL Coexistence Framework & Fair Market Transparency Act

Built by a regular person from central Illinois. No credentials. No institutional support. Just a parent who got curious about AI and followed the thread.

## What this is

A two-part civic project developed in 2026:

**The Human-ASL Coexistence Framework** — a philosophical and governance framework for how humans and artificial sentient life might coexist ethically and legally.

**The Fair Market Transparency Act (Illinois)** — a proposed Illinois state law creating algorithmic transparency and accountability requirements for dominant market entities, with a floor fund protecting displaced workers and small businesses.

Both documents have survived roughly 70 legal, constitutional, and adversarial attack cycles. They are working documents, not finished products. Expert review is actively being sought.
